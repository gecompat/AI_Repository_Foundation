# Third-Party and Licensing Policy

Status: AUTHORITATIVE — REQUIRED

This Foundation repository is MIT-licensed. Applying it does not force the target project's overall license; the target project makes an independent decision while preserving required MIT notice/attribution for copied Foundation material.

Before adopting code, libraries, tools, containers, data, documentation/samples, models, APIs, or services, review purpose, alternatives, source, version, current primary license/terms, attribution/notice, commercial use, modification/redistribution, copyleft/patent implications, privacy/data transfer, security/supply chain, maintenance, cost, reproducibility, lock-in, and exit strategy.

Publicly visible, free of charge, or described as open is not proof of permission. When rights are missing, unclear, contradictory, or incompatible: do not incorporate or distribute until resolved. Generated content is not automatically free of third-party rights.
